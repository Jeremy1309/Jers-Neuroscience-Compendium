About Me

My name is Jeremy Fumagalli, and I am the creator of Jer’s Neuroscience Compendium, a personal passion project dedicated to exploring the science of the brain, behaviour, and the biological processes that make us who we are.

I studied neuroscience at Carleton University from 2020 to 2024. I am especially interested in neuropharmacology and in the ways molecules, receptors, and neurons influence behaviour, cognition, and disease.

The compendium brings together knowledge developed through university study, scientific literature, and independent exploration. Its goal is to make neuroscience more approachable for me, my friends and family, and anyone else who is curious about how the nervous system works.

As the project grows, I plan to keep learning, sharing, and building clear explanations, diagrams, and interactive resources that help complex ideas feel more understandable. This is a living project rather than a finished textbook, and new topics and features will be added as my own understanding develops.

Outside of neuroscience, I enjoy basketball, rock climbing, going to the gym, and spending time with friends.

Thank you for visiting and exploring the fascinating world of neuroscience with me.
